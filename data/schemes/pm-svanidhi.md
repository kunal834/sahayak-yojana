## Overview
PM SVANidhi was launched in June 2020 by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to give street vendors affordable working-capital loans so they can restart and grow their livelihoods. In August 2025 the Union Cabinet approved restructuring and extending the scheme up to 31 March 2030.

## Benefits
- Collateral-free working-capital loans in three tranches: Rs 15,000 in the first tranche, Rs 25,000 in the second tranche and Rs 50,000 in the third tranche (enhanced from the earlier Rs 10,000, Rs 20,000 and Rs 50,000).
- A vendor becomes eligible for the next tranche after repaying the previous loan.
- Interest subsidy of 7% per year on timely or early repayment, credited to the bank account.
- Cashback incentives for doing digital transactions.
- A UPI-linked RuPay credit card for vendors who repay the second loan.

## Eligibility
- Street vendors engaged in vending in urban areas, including peri-urban and rural areas around cities.
- Vendors holding a Certificate of Vending or Identity Card issued by the Urban Local Body (ULB).
- Vendors identified in the ULB survey who have not been issued a certificate can get a provisional certificate.
- Vendors left out of the survey can apply with a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) issued by the ULB or Town Vending Committee.

## Documents Required
- Certificate of Vending / Identity Card, or Letter of Recommendation from the ULB.
- Aadhaar card linked with mobile number.
- Bank account details.
- Voter ID or other KYC document.

## How to Apply
Apply online on the PM SVANidhi portal (pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in), through a Common Service Centre, or with the help of the Urban Local Body or bank business correspondents.
