## Overview
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme of the Government of India, launched in February 2019 and run by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. It gives income support to landholding farmer families so they can meet expenses on agriculture and allied activities and domestic needs.

## Benefits
An eligible farmer family receives Rs 6,000 per year. The amount is paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 each, every four months. The money is transferred directly into the bank account of the beneficiary through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Eligibility
All landholding farmer families that own cultivable land in their name, as per the land records of the State or Union Territory, are eligible. A farmer family means husband, wife and minor children. The State or UT government identifies the eligible families.

## Exclusions
The following are NOT eligible for PM-KISAN:
- Institutional landholders.
- Farmer families in which one or more members hold or held a constitutional post.
- Former and present Ministers, Members of Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, State Legislative Assemblies and Councils, former and present Mayors of Municipal Corporations and Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of Central or State Government ministries, offices, departments, PSUs and local bodies (Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D employees are not excluded).
- Retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs 10,000 or more (Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D pensioners are not excluded).
- Persons who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
- Professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects registered with professional bodies and practising.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card (Aadhaar seeding is mandatory for payment).
- Land ownership records (khatauni / land record documents) of the State.
- Bank account details linked with Aadhaar.
- Mobile number.
e-KYC is mandatory for all PM-KISAN beneficiaries. It can be done through the OTP option on the PM-KISAN portal, biometric authentication at a Common Service Centre (CSC), or face authentication through the PM-KISAN mobile app.

## How to Apply
Farmers can register through the "New Farmer Registration" option on the PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in), through a Common Service Centre, or through the local Patwari / Revenue Officer / State Nodal Officer. Status of the application and payments can be checked under "Know Your Status" on the portal. Helpline: 155261 / 011-24300606.
