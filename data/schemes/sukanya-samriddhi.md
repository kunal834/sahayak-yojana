## Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a small savings scheme for the girl child launched in January 2015 under the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao campaign. Accounts can be opened at post offices and authorised banks.

## Benefits
- Attractive interest rate notified by the Government every quarter (higher than most small savings schemes).
- Deposits qualify for income tax deduction under Section 80C; interest and maturity amount are tax-free.
- Partial withdrawal of up to 50% of the balance is allowed after the girl turns 18 or passes Class 10, for higher education.

## Eligibility
- The account can be opened by a parent or legal guardian in the name of a girl child who is below 10 years of age.
- Only one account per girl child.
- A maximum of two accounts per family; a third account is allowed in case of twins or triplets born in the second birth or in the first birth.
- The girl must be a resident Indian.

## Deposit Rules
- Minimum deposit is Rs 250 per financial year; maximum is Rs 1.5 lakh per financial year.
- Deposits can be made for 15 years from the date of opening the account.
- The account matures 21 years from the date of opening.
- If the minimum deposit is not made, the account becomes a defaulted account and can be revived by paying Rs 50 penalty per year of default along with the minimum deposit.

## Documents Required
- Birth certificate of the girl child.
- Identity proof and address proof (such as Aadhaar and PAN) of the parent or guardian.
- Photographs of the parent or guardian and the girl child.
- Account opening form.

## How to Apply
Visit any post office or authorised bank branch, fill the Sukanya Samriddhi account opening form and submit it with the documents and the initial deposit.
