## Overview
PMMVY is a maternity benefit scheme of the Ministry of Women and Child Development. Under Mission Shakti (PMMVY 2.0, from 2022), it gives cash incentives to pregnant women and lactating mothers to partly compensate for wage loss and to improve health-seeking behaviour.

## Benefits
- For the first child: Rs 5,000 paid in two instalments - Rs 3,000 after registration of pregnancy and at least one ante-natal check-up within 6 months of the last menstrual period, and Rs 2,000 after the birth is registered and the child has received the first cycle of vaccinations.
- For the second child: Rs 6,000 in one instalment after birth, only if the second child is a girl, to promote a positive behaviour towards the girl child.
- Eligible beneficiaries also receive the remaining cash incentive under Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) for institutional delivery.

## Eligibility
Pregnant women and lactating mothers who belong to any of these groups:
- Women from SC or ST households.
- Women with a disability (40% or more).
- BPL ration card holders or beneficiaries under the National Food Security Act.
- Beneficiaries of Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.
- e-Shram card holders.
- Women farmers who are PM-KISAN beneficiaries.
- MGNREGA job card holders.
- Women whose net family income is less than Rs 8 lakh per year.
- Anganwadi Workers, Anganwadi Helpers and ASHA workers.
Women in regular employment with the Central or State Government or a PSU, or receiving similar benefits under any law, are not eligible.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card of the beneficiary (and of the husband, where applicable).
- Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card.
- Bank or post office account linked with Aadhaar.
- Proof of eligibility category (such as caste certificate, BPL card, e-Shram card, job card, or income certificate).
- Child's birth registration certificate (for the second instalment / second child).

## How to Apply
Register through the Anganwadi Worker or ASHA, at an approved health facility, or online on the PMMVY portal (pmmvy.wcd.gov.in). Registration should be done within 270 days of the last menstrual period.
