## Overview
PMSBY is a one-year accident insurance scheme, renewable every year, launched in May 2015. It provides affordable accident cover through banks and post offices.

## Benefits
- Rs 2 lakh for accidental death.
- Rs 2 lakh for total and irrecoverable loss of both eyes, both hands or both feet, or loss of sight of one eye and loss of use of one hand or foot.
- Rs 1 lakh for total and irrecoverable loss of sight of one eye or loss of use of one hand or foot.
- The premium is Rs 20 per year, auto-debited from the subscriber's bank account.
- The cover period is 1 June to 31 May every year.

## Eligibility
- Individuals aged 18 to 70 years who have a savings bank account or post office account.
- The person must give consent to join and to auto-debit the premium.

## Documents Required
- Savings bank account or post office account.
- Aadhaar card (primary KYC for the account).
- Consent-cum-declaration form.

## How to Apply
Enrol through your bank branch, post office, net banking, or the Jan Suraksha portal (jansuraksha.gov.in).
