## Overview
The Post Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste (SC) students is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. It provides financial help to SC students studying at the post-matriculation or post-secondary stage so they can complete their education.

## Benefits
- Payment of compulsory non-refundable fees charged by the educational institution (subject to limits set by fee-fixation committees of the State).
- Academic allowance per year, which varies by the course group and by whether the student is a hosteller or a day scholar.
- The Central share is paid directly into the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account through DBT.

## Eligibility
- The student must belong to a Scheduled Caste.
- The student must have passed matriculation (Class 10) or a higher examination and be studying in a recognised post-matric or post-secondary course (Class 11 onwards, including graduation, post-graduation, diploma, ITI and professional courses).
- The annual income of the parents or guardians from all sources must not exceed Rs 2.5 lakh.
- Students who are pursuing a course at the same level again (for example, a second graduation) are generally not eligible.

## Documents Required
- Caste certificate (SC) issued by the competent authority.
- Income certificate of parents / guardian.
- Marksheet of the last qualifying examination.
- Fee receipt / admission proof for the current course.
- Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-seeded bank account details.
- Domicile certificate of the State.

## How to Apply
Apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) or the State scholarship portal (for example, MahaDBT in Maharashtra) within the deadline announced each academic year. The institution and district officials verify the application.
