## Overview
PMFBY is a crop insurance scheme launched in 2016 by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. It protects farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities, pests and diseases.

## Benefits
- Insurance cover against yield loss from non-preventable risks such as drought, flood, inundation, pests, diseases, landslides, natural fire, lightning, storm, hailstorm and cyclone.
- Cover for prevented sowing, mid-season adversity, post-harvest losses (up to 14 days for crops kept in cut-and-spread condition) and localised calamities.
- Farmer premium is low: 2% of the sum insured for Kharif food and oilseed crops, 1.5% for Rabi food and oilseed crops, and 5% for commercial and horticultural crops. The balance premium is paid by the Central and State Governments.

## Eligibility
- All farmers, including sharecroppers and tenant farmers, growing notified crops in notified areas are eligible.
- Enrolment is voluntary for all farmers since Kharif 2020.
- Non-loanee farmers must submit documentary evidence of land records or of their tenancy or sharing agreement.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank account details (passbook copy).
- Land records (khatauni / land possession certificate) or tenancy / sharing agreement.
- Sowing certificate or self-declaration of the crop sown.

## How to Apply
Loanee farmers are covered through their bank. Non-loanee farmers can apply on the PMFBY portal (pmfby.gov.in), through a Common Service Centre, bank branch, or insurance company agent before the cut-off date of the season. Crop loss should be reported within 72 hours through the Crop Insurance app or helpline 14447.
