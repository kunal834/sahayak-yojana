## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana was launched in May 2016 by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas to provide clean cooking fuel (LPG) to women from poor households, replacing traditional fuels like firewood, coal and cow-dung cakes. Ujjwala 2.0 was launched in August 2021.

## Benefits
- A deposit-free LPG connection in the name of the adult woman of the household.
- Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first LPG refill and a hotplate (stove) are provided free of cost along with the connection.
- Targeted subsidy on LPG refills for PMUY beneficiaries, as notified by the Government from time to time.

## Eligibility
- The applicant must be a woman who has reached 18 years of age.
- There should be no other LPG connection in the same household.
- The applicant must belong to one of these categories: SC households, ST households, Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) beneficiaries, Most Backward Classes, Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) households, forest dwellers, residents of islands and river islands, tea and ex-tea garden tribes, or any poor household that submits a 14-point declaration.
- Migrant families can apply with a self-declaration of address instead of proof of address.

## Documents Required
- KYC form.
- Aadhaar card of the applicant.
- Aadhaar of the beneficiary and adult family members as listed in the ration card.
- Ration card issued by the State or a self-declaration (for migrants) showing family composition.
- Bank account number and IFSC code.
- Supplementary KYC as per the category the applicant belongs to.

## How to Apply
Apply online on the PMUY portal (pmuy.gov.in) or visit the nearest LPG distributor of Indian Oil (Indane), Bharat Petroleum or Hindustan Petroleum with the KYC documents.
