## Overview
The Kisan Credit Card scheme gives farmers timely and adequate short-term credit from banks for cultivation, post-harvest expenses, maintenance of farm assets and allied activities, through a single simple window. It was extended to animal husbandry and fisheries farmers in 2018-19.

## Benefits
- Revolving credit for crop production, post-harvest expenses, consumption needs of the farm household and working capital for allied activities.
- Interest subvention: short-term crop loans up to Rs 3 lakh are available at 7% interest per year, and farmers who repay promptly get an additional 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive, making the effective rate 4% per year. (The Union Budget 2025-26 announced an increase of this limit to Rs 5 lakh.)
- Collateral-free loans up to Rs 2 lakh (limit revised by RBI from 1 January 2025).
- KCC holders get a RuPay debit card and personal accident insurance cover.

## Eligibility
- All farmers - individuals or joint borrowers - who are owner cultivators.
- Tenant farmers, oral lessees and sharecroppers.
- Self Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers, including tenant farmers and sharecroppers.
- Farmers engaged in animal husbandry and fisheries (fishers, fish farmers, poultry, dairy and similar).

## Documents Required
- Duly filled application form.
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID or driving licence).
- Address proof.
- Land records / proof of cultivation (land holding documents) attested by the competent authority.
- Passport-size photographs.
- Cropping pattern details.

## How to Apply
Apply at any commercial bank, regional rural bank, small finance bank or cooperative bank. PM-KISAN beneficiaries can use the simplified one-page KCC form available on the PM-KISAN portal. Banks are expected to process KCC applications within 14 days.
