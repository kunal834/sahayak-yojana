## Overview
MGNREGA, enacted in 2005, guarantees wage employment to rural households. It is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development together with State Governments and Gram Panchayats.

## Benefits
- At least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.
- Wages are paid at the notified State-wise MGNREGA wage rate, directly into the worker's bank or post office account, within 15 days of completion of work.
- Work is to be provided within 5 km of the worker's residence; if it is farther, an extra 10% wage is paid for travel and living expenses.
- If work is not given within 15 days of applying, the applicant is entitled to an unemployment allowance.
- At least one-third of beneficiaries should be women.
- Worksite facilities such as drinking water, shade, first aid and creche are to be provided.

## Eligibility
- The household must reside in a rural area.
- Adult members (18 years or older) of the household who are willing to do unskilled manual work.
- The household must register with the Gram Panchayat and obtain a Job Card.

## Documents Required
- Application for registration to the Gram Panchayat (written or oral).
- Aadhaar card of adult members.
- Photographs of the adult members.
- Bank or post office account details.
- Proof of residence in the Gram Panchayat area.
The Gram Panchayat issues the Job Card free of cost within 15 days of the application.

## How to Apply
Register the household at the Gram Panchayat to get a Job Card. Then submit a written application for work (stating the period and number of days) to the Gram Panchayat or Programme Officer and collect a dated receipt.
