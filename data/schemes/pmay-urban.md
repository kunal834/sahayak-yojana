## Overview
PMAY-Urban 2.0 was approved by the Union Cabinet in August 2024 to help 1 crore urban poor and middle-class families build, buy or rent a pucca house at an affordable cost over five years. It is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

## Benefits
The scheme has four verticals:
- Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC): financial assistance to eligible families to build a new house on their own land.
- Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP): houses built by States, cities or private agencies offered to eligible families.
- Affordable Rental Housing (ARH): rental housing for urban migrants, working women, industrial workers and similar groups.
- Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS): interest subsidy on home loans for EWS, LIG and MIG families.
Under BLC and AHP, the Government assistance is up to Rs 2.5 lakh per housing unit.

## Eligibility
- Economically Weaker Section (EWS): annual household income up to Rs 3 lakh.
- Low Income Group (LIG): annual household income above Rs 3 lakh and up to Rs 6 lakh.
- Middle Income Group (MIG): annual household income above Rs 6 lakh and up to Rs 9 lakh.
- The family must not own a pucca house anywhere in India.
- A family includes husband, wife and unmarried children.
- Families who have already taken benefit of a housing scheme of the Central or State Government in the last 20 years are not eligible.
- Widows, single women, persons with disabilities, senior citizens, transgenders, SC/ST, minorities and other weaker sections are given preference.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card of all family members.
- Income certificate or self-declaration of income.
- Bank account details linked with Aadhaar.
- Proof of land ownership (for the BLC vertical).
- Self-declaration that the family does not own a pucca house anywhere in India.

## How to Apply
Apply online through the PMAY-U 2.0 Unified Web Portal (pmay-urban.gov.in) or through the Urban Local Body / Common Service Centre.
