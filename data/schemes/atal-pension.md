## Overview
Atal Pension Yojana is a pension scheme launched in May 2015 focused mainly on workers in the unorganised sector. It is administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA).

## Benefits
- Guaranteed minimum monthly pension of Rs 1,000, Rs 2,000, Rs 3,000, Rs 4,000 or Rs 5,000 after the age of 60, depending on the contribution chosen.
- After the subscriber's death, the same pension is paid to the spouse.
- After the death of both the subscriber and the spouse, the accumulated pension wealth is returned to the nominee.
- The monthly contribution depends on the age at joining and the pension amount chosen; joining early means a lower contribution.

## Eligibility
- Any citizen of India aged between 18 and 40 years.
- The applicant must have a savings bank account or a post office savings account.
- From 1 October 2022, any citizen who is or has been an income-tax payer is not eligible to join APY.
- The subscriber must contribute until the age of 60.

## Documents Required
- Savings bank account (Aadhaar-linked preferred).
- Aadhaar card.
- Active mobile number.
- APY registration form.

## How to Apply
Visit the bank branch or post office where you hold a savings account and fill the APY registration form, or register through the net-banking or mobile banking app of your bank. Contributions are auto-debited from the savings account monthly, quarterly or half-yearly.
