## Overview
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, launched in September 2018 and implemented by the National Health Authority (NHA), is one of the largest government-funded health assurance schemes in the world. It provides cashless and paperless treatment at empanelled public and private hospitals across India.

## Benefits
- Health cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation.
- No cap on family size or on the age of members.
- Pre-existing diseases are covered from the first day.
- Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalisation and 15 days of post-hospitalisation expenses, including diagnostics and medicines.
- Benefits are portable: a beneficiary can get treatment at any empanelled hospital anywhere in India.
- Services are cashless at the point of care.

## Eligibility
- Rural families are identified using deprivation criteria from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011, for example households with only one room with kutcha walls and roof, no adult member between 16 and 59, female-headed households with no adult male member between 16 and 59, households with a disabled member and no able-bodied adult, SC/ST households, and landless households earning mainly from manual casual labour.
- Urban families are identified through 11 occupational criteria such as rag pickers, beggars, domestic workers, street vendors, construction workers, plumbers, masons, painters, welders, security guards, sanitation workers, rickshaw pullers, transport workers, and similar workers.
- Families covered under the Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY) are also included.
- Senior citizens aged 70 years and above: since October 2024, all senior citizens aged 70 and above are eligible irrespective of income, under the Ayushman Vay Vandana card. They get a separate top-up cover of up to Rs 5 lakh per year.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card or other government photo ID.
- Ration card or proof of family composition.
- Mobile number.
Eligibility can be checked on the "Am I Eligible" page (beneficiary.nha.gov.in) using a mobile number, or at an empanelled hospital through the Ayushman Mitra help desk.

## How to Apply
There is no separate enrolment for SECC-identified families. Eligible beneficiaries obtain an Ayushman Card through the Ayushman app, the beneficiary portal (beneficiary.nha.gov.in), a Common Service Centre, or an empanelled hospital after Aadhaar-based e-KYC. Helpline: 14555.
