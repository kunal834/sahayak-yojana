## Overview
The Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (CSSS) of the Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education, gives financial help to meritorious students from low-income families to meet part of their day-to-day expenses while studying in college and university.

## Benefits
- Rs 12,000 per year at graduation level for the first three years of college and university courses.
- Rs 20,000 per year at post-graduation level.
- Students in professional courses get Rs 20,000 per year in the 4th and 5th years.
- A total of 82,000 fresh scholarships are given each year (41,000 for boys and 41,000 for girls), divided among States based on population in the 18-25 age group.

## Eligibility
- The student must be above the 80th percentile of successful candidates in the relevant stream of the Class 12 board examination.
- The student must be pursuing a regular degree course (not correspondence or distance mode, and not a diploma course) in a recognised college or institution.
- The gross annual family income must be up to Rs 4.5 lakh.
- Students already availing benefits of any other scholarship scheme are not eligible.

## Documents Required
- Class 12 marksheet.
- Family income certificate.
- Admission proof / fee receipt of the current course.
- Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-seeded bank account details.
- Caste certificate, if applicable for reservation.

## How to Apply
Apply online on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in). The scholarship must be renewed every year on the portal, and renewal requires at least 50% marks in the previous annual examination and adequate attendance.
