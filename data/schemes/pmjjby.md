## Overview
PMJJBY is a one-year life insurance scheme, renewable every year, launched in May 2015. It is offered through banks and post offices with LIC and other life insurance companies.

## Benefits
- Life cover of Rs 2 lakh in case of death of the insured due to any reason.
- The premium is Rs 436 per year, auto-debited from the subscriber's bank account.
- The cover period is 1 June to 31 May every year.

## Eligibility
- Individuals aged 18 to 50 years who have a savings bank account or post office account.
- The person must give consent to join and to auto-debit the premium.
- Persons who join before 50 can continue the cover up to the age of 55 by paying the premium.
- For new enrolments, there is a lien period of 30 days from enrolment during which death claims (except due to accident) are not payable.

## Documents Required
- Savings bank account or post office account.
- Aadhaar card (primary KYC for the account).
- Consent-cum-declaration form.

## How to Apply
Enrol through your bank branch, post office, net banking, or the Jan Suraksha portal (jansuraksha.gov.in) by submitting the consent-cum-declaration form.
