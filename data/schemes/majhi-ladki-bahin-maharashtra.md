## Overview
Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana is a scheme of the Government of Maharashtra, launched in July 2024 by the Women and Child Development Department, to improve the financial independence, health and nutrition of women in the State.

## Benefits
- Rs 1,500 per month is transferred directly into the eligible woman's Aadhaar-linked bank account through DBT.

## Eligibility
- The applicant must be a resident (domicile) of Maharashtra.
- Married, widowed, divorced, abandoned or destitute women, and one unmarried woman per family.
- Age between 21 and 65 years.
- The annual family income must not exceed Rs 2.5 lakh.
- The applicant must have a bank account linked with Aadhaar.

## Exclusions
- Families with a member who is an income-tax payer.
- Families with a member who is a regular or permanent employee or a pensioner of the Central or State Government, a government undertaking or a local body.
- Families with a member who is a current or former MP or MLA.
- Families that own a four-wheeler (tractors are excluded from this condition).
- Women already receiving Rs 1,500 or more per month under another government scheme.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card.
- Maharashtra domicile certificate (or birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or ration card / voter ID of 15 years as alternatives).
- Income certificate (yellow or orange ration card holders are exempt from the income certificate).
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked).
- Ration card.
- Passport-size photograph.
- Self-declaration form.

## How to Apply
Apply online on the official portal (ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in), through the Nari Shakti Doot app, or offline at the Anganwadi centre, Gram Panchayat, ward office or Setu Suvidha Kendra.
