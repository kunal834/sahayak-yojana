## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana was launched in April 2015 to provide collateral-free loans to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises for income-generating activities in manufacturing, trading, services and activities allied to agriculture (such as poultry, dairy and beekeeping).

## Benefits
Loans are given under four categories:
- Shishu: loans up to Rs 50,000.
- Kishore: loans above Rs 50,000 and up to Rs 5 lakh.
- Tarun: loans above Rs 5 lakh and up to Rs 10 lakh.
- Tarun Plus: loans above Rs 10 lakh and up to Rs 20 lakh, for entrepreneurs who have availed and successfully repaid previous loans under the Tarun category (introduced in the Union Budget 2024-25).
No collateral is required. Loans are covered under the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU).

## Eligibility
- Any Indian citizen who has a business plan for a non-farm income-generating activity such as manufacturing, processing, trading or services.
- The credit need must be up to Rs 20 lakh (Tarun Plus only for previous Tarun borrowers).
- The applicant should not be a defaulter to any bank or financial institution and should have a satisfactory credit track record.
- Shopkeepers, small manufacturers, fruit and vegetable vendors, artisans, food-service units, repair shops, truck operators and similar micro units are covered.

## Documents Required
- Identity proof and address proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN and similar).
- Passport-size photographs.
- Business plan / quotation of machinery or items to be purchased.
- Proof of business identity and address (registration certificate, licence, Udyam registration if available).
- Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC), if applicable.
- For Kishore and Tarun: bank statements and income tax returns / balance sheets, as applicable.

## How to Apply
Apply at any public sector bank, private bank, regional rural bank, small finance bank, cooperative bank, MFI or NBFC, or online through the Udyamimitra portal (udyamimitra.in).
